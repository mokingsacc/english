First Pass · roundabouts

Questions before the build

First Pass is a browser tool for trying a roundabout idea in minutes: checks like fastest path speeds and sight distance, before you build it properly in Civil 3D. Your answers decide what it does first and how it works. Part 1 takes about two minutes. Tap your answers, add a note anywhere, then press Copy my answers at the bottom and send the text back to Mo.
